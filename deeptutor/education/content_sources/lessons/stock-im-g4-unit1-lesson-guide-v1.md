## 第1课：Multiples of a Number

课时编号：IM:G4:U1:L1。

教学目的（原文）：The purpose of this lesson is for students to apply their understanding of area to explore multiples.

教师目标（原文）：
Find areas of different rectangles with a given a side length.

Understand that the area of a rectangle is a multiple of each of its side lengths.

学生目标（原文）：Let’s build some rectangles.
